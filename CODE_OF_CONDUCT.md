# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and patient. People here volunteer their time, and many are new to
  open source or writing in a second language.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree respectfully.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content, or sharing other
  people's private information.
- Don't use the project to share pirated books.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer
([@sthrnilshaaa](https://github.com/sthrnilshaaa)) privately through GitHub. Reports are
kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block
people who break these rules.
