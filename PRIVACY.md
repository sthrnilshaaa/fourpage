# Privacy Policy

**Fourpages** (Android app `com.fourpages.reader`)
Effective: 19 October 2026

Fourpages is a free, open-source book reader. It has no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Data that stays on your device

Everything you put into Fourpages is stored only on your phone, inside the
app's private storage:

- the books and documents you import, and their covers;
- reading progress, bookmarks, highlights and notes;
- shelves, reading statistics and settings.

The developer never receives any of it. It is removed when you uninstall the
app or use **Settings › Reset library**.

## When the app uses the internet

The app only goes online for these optional features:

| Feature | What is sent | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Define** (look up a word you selected) | The single word, in the request address | [Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev) (`api.dictionaryapi.dev`) |
| **Update check**, Google Play version | Handled by Google Play on your device | Google ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) |
| **Update check**, GitHub version | A request for the latest release (no personal data) | GitHub ([GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)) |

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address.
Fourpages sends no identifiers, account data or book contents. All connections
use HTTPS.

Links you tap yourself (Ko-fi, GitHub, Google Play, sharing a quote) open in
your browser or the app you choose, and that service's own privacy policy
applies.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet / network state | The optional features above |
| Notifications | Reading reminders you turn on in Settings |
| Run at startup (boot completed) | Re-arming those reminders after a restart |
| Storage / folder access | Importing books from files or folders you pick |
| Install apps (GitHub version only) | Installing an update you chose to download |

## Children

Fourpages is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.

## Data sharing and selling

None. The developer collects no personal data, so there is nothing to share
or sell.
